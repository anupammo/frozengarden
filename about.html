Our story

A garden we built
to be still in.

Frost meets blossom — the idea behind everything we're building in Gutri, Deuli.

Gutri, Deuli · South 24 Parganas

Eighteen rooms and villas set around a working garden and a still pond, forty-five minutes from the city edge.

Why we started

Named for a
contradiction.

The Frozen Garden started as a question: could a place feel both still and alive at once? Frost and bloom don't usually belong in the same sentence — one holds everything in place, the other keeps growing anyway. That tension is the whole idea.

We found the land in Gutri almost by accident — a stretch of pond and open field on the edge of Deuli, quiet in a way that's hard to describe until you've stood in it. Rather than build something loud on top of it, we built around what was already there: the water, the working fields, the slow pace of a village morning.

Every room, every meal, every path through the garden is meant to hold that same tension — calm enough to rest in, alive enough to remember.

What guides us

Four things we don't compromise on.

Keep the land as it is

No landscape flattened or paved over to look "resort-like." What's native stays.

Hire from the village

Most of our team is local to Deuli — it's their place too, not just ours.

Cook what's in season

No fixed menu flown in from elsewhere — the kitchen follows the garden.

Protect the quiet

We cap how many guests stay at once. Quiet is the whole point — we won't crowd it out.
