On the property

Room to play,
room to rest.

A swimming pool, boating, open-air huts, wide lawns and an air-conditioned restaurant, all inside one quiet village resort.

Swimming pool

Tiled pool in the middle of the resort. Open until 7 PM.

Boating

Pedal boats on the water channels between the huts.

Open-air huts

Thatched huts with benches for your group, free with every package.

Restaurant

Air-conditioned dining hall with garden views.

Gardens and lawns

Flower-lined paths and big lawns for games.

Mud houses

Thatched mud houses with A/C beside the water.

Rooms with a view

Balconies over the gardens in the main building.

Gazebos

Thatched gazebos by the lawn and on the pond.
