Getting to Gutri, Deuli.

South 24 Parganas, West Bengal

About an hour from central Kolkata — close enough for a day out, far enough to feel like it isn't.

By roadSelf-drive or cab via Baruipur–Jibantala road; on-site parking is free for guests.
By trainNearest station is Jibantala; we can arrange a pickup with advance notice.
