While you're here

A few ways to
spend the day.

Nothing scheduled to the minute — just a handful of things worth doing between long stretches of doing nothing at all.

01
Sunrise

Morning walks

Guided walks through the paddy fields at sunrise, led by someone from the village who knows every bund and shortcut. Slow, quiet, and over before the day gets warm.

~45 minutes
Small groups
Daily, sunrise
02
Hands in the soil

Garden workshops

Seasonal planting sessions with our own gardeners — what to sow, when, and why the beds are laid out the way they are. Take a cutting back with you if you'd like.

~1 hour
All ages
On request
03
Golden hour

Boat rides

Short rides across the pond as the light turns gold — just the water, the reeds, and whoever's rowing. Our quietest thirty minutes of the day.

~30 minutes
Up to 3 at a time
Evenings, weather permitting
04
After dinner

Bonfire nights

Stories, snacks and stargazing around the fire once the kitchen winds down. No agenda — people tend to stay long after they meant to go to bed.

Until it burns out
Open to everyone staying
Nightly
Good to know

Before you plan your day.

A few common questions about the experiences on offer.

Morning walks and the bonfire run daily and need no booking — just show up. Garden workshops and boat rides are on request, so let our team know a day ahead so we can plan around the weather and group size.

All four experiences are included for guests staying with us — no separate charge. We keep the list short on purpose rather than upselling an activity menu.

Yes, on both counts. The walks are flat and unhurried, and the boat rides are calm, still-water crossings with a guide rowing — we pace either around whoever's joining.

The boat rides and bonfire are weather-dependent and get paused or moved under cover. Morning walks and garden workshops usually continue in light rain — check with the caretaker team on the day.
