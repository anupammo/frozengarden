The place

A village resort built around stillness.

The Frozen Garden sits in Gutri, on the edge of Deuli — a stretch of open sky, working fields, and quiet water. We built it for people who want a real rest: no crowds, no noise, just a garden, a fire, and time.

About an hour from central Kolkata

Close enough for a weekend, far enough to feel like it isn't.

Working village setting

Ponds, paddy and native gardens — kept as they are, not staged.

Slow food, local kitchen

Meals cooked to order from what's in season nearby.
