Good to know

Before you book.

The short version of our terms.

Night stay: check-in 11:00 AM, check-out 10:00 AM the next day. Day outing: 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability.

Children under 6 are free and share from the common buffet. Children from 6 to 10 pay the child rate shown above. From 11 years, the adult rate applies.

Yes. Every price on this site and on your quote includes GST. Your invoice shows the GST inside each amount.

Get a quote online, then pay a 50% advance by UPI or bank transfer. The balance is paid at the resort before check-out. You get a booking page, an invoice and a receipt for every payment.

Cancel 10 days or more before your visit and the advance is refunded, less 15%. Later cancellations and no-shows are not refunded. We'll usually move your date instead if you ask early. Full policy.

Pets (except guide dogs) and outside food aren't allowed. Every package includes the meals listed, served as a common buffet.
